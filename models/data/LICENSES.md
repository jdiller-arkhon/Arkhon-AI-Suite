# Training and eval data license ledger

**Hard rule:** no training or fine-tuning data may be authored, generated or paraphrased by Claude.
Allowed sources:
1. Permissively licensed public datasets, verified and recorded below.
2. Data supplied by the owner or by design partners under written permission. Reference the signed agreement ID; never commit the data itself.
3. Synthetic data from open-weight teacher models whose licenses allow it. Record the teacher, its license, and the generation config.

Every dataset needs a row **before** it is downloaded into any pipeline. The pipeline in Phase 7
refuses datasets that have no row here.
