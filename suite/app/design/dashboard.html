            Help

            Short answers to common questions, and how to reach a person.

            Does my customers' information leave this computer?

            No. Arkhon reads your email and files and runs its AI right here, on this computer. It only connects to the apps you connected, to read new items and to send what you approved. The Privacy Center lists every connection it made.

            Will Arkhon send anything on its own?

            No. Every email, quote and bill waits for a person to press Send or File. You can change the text first, or dismiss it and nothing happens.

            What does "Not sure" or "Not found in your data" mean?

            Arkhon couldn't find the answer in your files, so it didn't guess. Answer it once and Arkhon remembers it for next time.

            How do I add someone or change what they can use?

            Go to Company → People to invite someone and pick their role. Teams add extra tools to a group of people without a new role.

            How is "time saved" counted?

            Only from work someone approved. Each kind of work counts a set number of minutes, listed in the Value report. Owners and admins can change those numbers.

            An app says "Sign in again". What do I do?

            Open Company → Connections and press Fix next to it. Arkhon pauses that app until you sign in again; nothing is lost.

            How do I start over or remove Arkhon's data?

            An owner or admin can delete everything Arkhon read and learned from the Privacy Center. People and roles stay so you can still sign in.

            Talk to a person

            Arkhon support can help with setup, connections and anything that seems off.

            Write to Arkhon support through the contact page at arkhonindustries.com. Tell us what you were doing and what you expected to see.

            Support never needs your customers' information, and we'll never ask for passwords. If you want to share a screenshot, cover anything private first.
