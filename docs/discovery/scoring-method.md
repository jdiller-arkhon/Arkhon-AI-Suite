# Workspace prioritization scoring

Each workspace gets a score per interview. Scores are then averaged across interviews, separately
for each industry.

**Score = Pain × Frequency weight × WTP factor × Commitment multiplier**

- **Pain (1–5):** what the interviewer rated for that workspace's job, based on time lost and the frustration the owner expressed. Owner adjectives don't count.
- **Frequency weight:** daily 1.0 · weekly 0.7 · monthly 0.4.
- **WTP factor:** stated annual willingness to pay ÷ the median across all interviews, capped at 2.0.
- **Commitment multiplier:** 1 + 0.25 × commitment score (0–4). Commitments outweigh opinions.

Rank the workspaces by mean score. A workspace in the top 3 that also scores ≥ 4 on privacy concern
is a launch candidate. Use the per-industry means to confirm or overturn the launch Industry Pack
choice. **Minimum sample before we trust a ranking:** 12 interviews, at least 4 per candidate
industry.

Pricing tiers (Starter / Business / Pro) are revisited once 12 interviews are in. If Desk or Meetings
outranks Docs, we propose moving it into Starter.
