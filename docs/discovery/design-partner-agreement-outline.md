# Design-partner agreement — OUTLINE

> ⚠️ **DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. NOT A CONTRACT. DO NOT SIGN OR SEND.**
> This outline lists the issues an attorney should cover. It is not legal advice.

1. **Parties and term:** Arkhon Industries and the partner. Pilot length (e.g., 90 days), renewable.
2. **What the partner gets:** a free or discounted license during the pilot, priority support, and locked founder pricing for N years.
3. **What Arkhon gets:** structured feedback (interviews, surveys), usage metrics, and optionally data permission (§4). Optionally a reference or case study, with separate written consent.
4. **Data use permission (core section):**
   - 4.1 **Default: no data leaves the partner.** The software runs locally. Arkhon receives no content.
   - 4.2 **Optional, opt-in by category:** the partner may share specific *de-identified* documents or emails for (a) building evaluation sets and/or (b) training or fine-tuning Arkhon engines. Each purpose gets its own checkbox.
   - 4.3 **Scope schedule:** exact datasets, date ranges, and excluded categories (PHI, privileged material, CUI/ITAR, payment card data, personal data of minors).
   - 4.4 **De-identification:** who performs it, the method, and whether the partner reviews before transfer.
   - 4.5 **Security:** encrypted transfer and storage, access limited to named personnel, no third-party AI services process the data, retention limit, deletion on request with certification.
   - 4.6 **Ownership:** the partner keeps ownership of its data. Arkhon owns models and weights trained with it. The partner gets a license to resulting improvements.
   - 4.7 **Withdrawal:** the partner may revoke permission going forward. Attorney to address what happens to models already trained.
   - 4.8 **Privacy law:** controller/processor roles, GDPR/CCPA/state law, and a BAA where HIPAA applies (healthcare pack).
5. **Confidentiality:** mutual NDA covering roadmap, pricing and partner data.
6. **Feedback license:** Arkhon may use suggestions freely.
7. **Pre-release disclaimer:** beta software, AS-IS, outputs are drafts that need human review, no professional advice.
8. **Liability:** caps and exclusions. The partner keeps responsibility for anything it sends.
9. **Publicity:** no use of name or logo without written approval.
10. **Termination and post-term:** data return or deletion, license conversion.
11. **Governing law and venue.**

Attorney questions: Is a single opt-in sufficient for training use, or is per-dataset consent
needed? Do employee emails require employee notice? Are there special terms for government or
defense subcontractors?
