# Owner interview script (30 minutes)

**Goal:** learn how owners actually work today, not whether they like our idea. Ask about past
behavior and real costs. Never pitch before minute 25. Record answers in `scoring-sheet.csv`.
Get consent to take notes, and don't record audio unless the owner agrees in writing.

**Rules:** ask about the last specific time something happened ("Tell me about the last time…"),
not hypotheticals ("Would you use…"). A compliment is not data. A commitment (time, data, money,
an intro) is.

## 0. Opening (2 min)
- Thank them, explain the purpose: "We're learning how businesses like yours handle email,
  documents and customer questions. No sales pitch today."
- Company size, industry, the owner's role, and who does admin work.

## 1. Workload map (6 min) — N7, N8, N3
1. Walk me through yesterday. Where did the hours go that weren't the "real work"?
2. Roughly how many emails a day do you, and your team, write or answer? Which ones are repetitive?
3. Who answers customer questions? What do they look up to answer them?
4. What paperwork comes in (invoices, receipts, forms, contracts)? Who keys it in, and where to?
5. Which software do you pay for today? Which AI tools, if any, and what do they cost each month?

## 2. AI experience (5 min) — N2, N4, N3
6. Tell me about the last time you or an employee used an AI tool for work. What happened?
7. Has an AI tool ever given you a wrong answer that mattered? What did you do?
8. Who set it up? How long did that take?

## 3. Data and trust (6 min) — N1, N4, N9
9. Is there information you would never paste into ChatGPT or a similar tool? Why?
10. Do you have client, regulatory or contract obligations about where data lives (HIPAA, CMMC/ITAR, legal privilege, NDAs)?
11. If software could draft replies and file documents, what would you need to see before letting it send or change anything?
12. Would you want it to act on its own for any task? Which ones?

## 4. Value and money (6 min) — N5, N10
13. If 5 hours a week of admin disappeared, what would that be worth to you? How would you know it happened?
14. How do you prefer to buy software: monthly, annual, one-time? Has usage-based pricing ever burned you?
15. Would you buy a ~$1,500–2,500 PC or box if it ran everything privately in your office? Who approves that spend?
16. What did you last spend on a tool to save time? How did you justify it?

## 5. Fit (3 min) — N11, N6
17. Which systems must anything new work with (email, files, QuickBooks/Xero, CRM, calendar)?
18. What industry words or documents would prove to you that a tool "gets" your business?

## 6. Close (2 min) — commitment test
- Short concept (2 sentences, local and private, drafts, owner approves). Then: "Would you try a 14-day pilot on your own email and files?"
- Ask for: a pilot slot, sample documents (under the design-partner agreement), and an intro to a peer.
- Score the commitment level: 0 none · 1 follow-up OK · 2 pilot yes · 3 data + pilot · 4 pre-pay or LOI.
